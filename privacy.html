1. Introduction & Scope

1.1This Privacy Policy describes how Planet AllMatt ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use Hermes OS, a content-management and publishing platform that provides AI regulation compliance documentation, TikTok creator integration, commerce capabilities, and automated monitoring services (the "Platform").

1.2This Privacy Policy applies to your use of the Platform, including our GitHub Pages site, Supabase Edge Functions, Cloudflare Workers, TikTok Login Kit integration, and related services. This policy does not apply to TikTok's own data practices — please review TikTok's Privacy Policy for information about how TikTok handles your data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you interact with our Platform, we collect the following information:

  • Form Submission Data — When you submit forms on our waitlist, gap scan, or format kit download pages, we collect: your email address, business name, business website, AI use case information, jurisdictions, session duration, IP address, and user agent string.
  • TikTok Authentication Data — When you connect your TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit, we receive your TikTok user ID and an access token. We do not store your TikTok password.
  • Payment Information — When you make a purchase via our Stripe integration, Stripe collects your payment information directly. We do not store your full payment card details. We receive the transaction amount, currency, and transaction ID for accounting purposes only.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Usage Data — We automatically collect information about your interaction with the Platform, including pages visited, time spent, referring URLs, and device information (IP address, browser type, operating system).
  • Cookies & Tracking — We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Platform. These are used for session management and analytics. No third-party advertising cookies are used.
  • TikTok Webhook Events — We receive TikTok webhook notifications for your connected TikTok account, including video views, likes, comments, shares, profile views, clicks, and conversion events. These events are stored in our Cloudflare Workers KV namespace and Supabase database.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • TikTok — When you authorize Hermes OS via TikTok Login Kit, TikTok shares your user profile information (user ID, display name, avatar, follower count) with us as authorized by your consent grant.
  • Stripe — We receive payment status updates and webhook notifications about transactions processed through our Stripe account.
  • GitHub Pages — Standard web server logs (IP address, browser, referring page) are generated when you access our GitHub Pages site.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Platform Operations — To authenticate your account, manage your TikTok integration, publish content on your behalf, and store your data.
  • Analytics & Metrics — To measure Platform usage, track engagement metrics (including TikTok video performance), and generate reports for our business intelligence system (Nightshift monitoring).
  • Communication — To send you email confirmations for form submissions, service updates, and relevant business communications via Resend email service.
  • Commerce — To process payments for products and services, generate Stripe Checkout Sessions, and manage transactions.
  • Research & Development — To improve the AI Regulation Readiness Toolkit and other Platform features based on usage patterns and qualified demand signals.
  • Legal Compliance — To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • TikTok — We share data with TikTok only as necessary to provide the Platform's TikTok Login Kit and content publishing features. TikTok processes this data according to their own privacy policy.
  • Stripe — We share payment-related information with Stripe solely for processing transactions. Stripe is contractually obligated to protect your payment data.
  • Resend — We share your email address with Resend solely for sending email confirmations and notifications.
  • Supabase — We store your form submission data and TikTok analytics metrics in Supabase databases for operational and analytical purposes.
  • Cloudflare — We use Cloudflare Workers KV for temporary event storage. Data in KV is not used for advertising or profiling.
  • Service Providers — We may share your data with trusted service providers who assist us in operating the Platform, conducting business, or serving you, provided they agree to keep your information confidential.
  • Legal Requirements — We may disclose your information when required by law, to comply with legal processes, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our Platform, users, or others.
Important: TikTok webhook events are sent exclusively to our webhook endpoint at https://hermes-tiktok-webhook.matthew-mickens1.workers.dev/webhook/tiktok. We do not share your TikTok data with third parties beyond what is necessary for Platform operation.

5. Data Retention & Storage

5.1We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the Platform's services and for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Form submission data — Retained in our Supabase database for analytics and business follow-up.
  • TikTok webhook events — Stored in Cloudflare KV (temporary) and Supabase (permanent for analytics).
  • TikTok access tokens — Stored securely; you may revoke access at any time via TikTok or our app.
  • Stripe transaction records — Retained for accounting and compliance purposes as required by law.

5.2You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will delete your data within 30 days of such request, except where we need to retain it for legal or business purposes (e.g., Stripe transaction records for tax compliance).

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Portability — You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — You can request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal exceptions).
  • Opt-Out of Marketing — You may opt out of receiving marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link in our emails.
  • TikTok Revocation — You may revoke Hermes OS's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok account settings.
  • Stripe Data — Stripe handles your payment data directly; please review Stripe's Privacy Policy for your payment-related rights.

7. Security

7.1We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes using HTTPS/TLS for all data transmission, secure storage for API credentials (via Windows User environment variables and Supabase secrets), and following security best practices for our Cloudflare Workers, Supabase Edge Functions, and Supabase database.

7.2No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Data Transfers

8.1Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. Our infrastructure (GitHub Pages, Supabase, Cloudflare, Stripe, Resend) operates in the United States and other jurisdictions. By using the Platform, you consent to these transfers.

9. Children's Privacy

9.1The Platform is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.
